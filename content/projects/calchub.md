### Project Overview
**CalcHubBD** is an all-in-one calculation platform housing over 60 essential tools for both daily life and professional workflows. The platform is uniquely engineered to align with localized Bangladeshi standards, such as specific taxation rules and regional unit conversions, making it a highly relevant tool for the local market.

### Core Features
* **60+ Specialized Tools:**
    * **Financial:** Advanced Loan EMI calculators, FD/RD return estimators, and localized VAT/Tax calculation engines.
    * **Health & Wellness:** Tools for tracking BMI, BMR, and daily water intake requirements.
    * **Academic:** Precise GPA/CGPA calculators and comprehensive unit conversion tools.
    * **Business Logic:** Professional tools for calculating profit margins, markups, and inventory metrics.
* **Localized Calculation Logic:** Beyond global standards, the platform integrates regional currency formats and specific taxation laws applicable in Bangladesh.
* **Smart Global Search:** Features an instant-discovery search system to help users find specific tools without navigating through complex menus.
* **Responsive Architecture:** Built with **Next.js** and **Tailwind CSS** to ensure a seamless, high-performance experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

### Technical Stack
* **Frontend:** **Next.js (App Router)** for optimized server-side rendering and high performance.
* **Styling:** **Tailwind CSS** for a modern, utility-first design approach.
* **Deployment:** Hosted on **Vercel**, optimized for lightning-fast loading speeds and global availability.

### Why I Built This
Daily tasks often require switching between multiple apps or websites for simple calculations—whether it's checking a bank loan EMI or a student calculating their GPA. **CalcHubBD** was created to eliminate this friction by consolidating all these essential tools into a single, high-speed, and reliable platform.
